Pre-Debut Story

Bonnie discovered dance through a children's performance academy and later began training in both dance and vocals. She auditioned for Starship Entertainment after participating in an international youth performance event.

As the youngest trainee in PANDORA's lineup, Bonnie was initially considered inexperienced, but her rapid improvement surprised the trainers. Her bright personality and strong dancing eventually made her a natural choice for the group's maknae.
