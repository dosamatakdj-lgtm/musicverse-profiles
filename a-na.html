About A-na

A-na is one of ELLEVEN's youngest members and contributes to both the group's vocal and visual lineup.


Her position allows her to take supporting vocal parts while also contributing to ELLEVEN's overall visual identity.


A-na is also associated with the group's fashion activities and has been selected as a representative for Dior.
