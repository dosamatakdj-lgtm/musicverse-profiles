Pre-Debut Story

Hinari started dancing in Japan and later became interested in hip-hop and rap. After passing an international audition, she moved to Korea to begin trainee life.

She initially struggled with the language and unfamiliar training system, but quickly became known for her confidence and energetic performances. Her combination of rap and dance eventually earned her a place in PANDORA.
