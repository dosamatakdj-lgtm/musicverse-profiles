Pre-Debut Story

Jiwoo began training after being discovered at a local youth dance competition. She originally auditioned for Starship Entertainment as a vocalist, but her strong stage presence quickly caught the attention of the dance trainers as well.

During her trainee years, Jiwoo became known for being one of the most dependable members in monthly evaluations. She often helped younger trainees learn choreography and was eventually chosen as one of the first members of PANDORA's debut lineup.
