Pre-Debut Story

Jeemin became interested in performing after joining a school dance club at a young age. She later auditioned for Starship Entertainment after a staff member noticed her at a dance showcase.

As a trainee, Jeemin stood out for her camera presence and expressive facial expressions. Although she initially focused mainly on dance, she gradually trained in rap and became one of PANDORA's most recognizable members during pre-debut evaluations.
