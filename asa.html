About Asa

Asa is one of ELLEVEN's defining performers. Her greatest strengths are rap and dance, making her one of the group's most performance-focused members.


Her rap level places her at the top of ELLEVEN's rap line, while her dance ability allows her to handle some of the group's most demanding choreography.


Despite being one of the younger members compared with Ruka and Pharita, Asa has a strong stage presence and contributes a powerful energy to ELLEVEN's performances.
