About Yuha

Yuha is the fifth oldest member of ELLEVEN and serves as one of the group's Main Vocalists. Known for her strong vocal ability and stable performances, she brings a powerful and polished vocal color to ELLEVEN. Her confidence and consistency make her one of the group's key vocalists.

ELLEVEN

ELLEVEN is an 11-member girl group under Aurelia Entertainment. The group debuted with Lion and is known for its powerful performances, strong vocals, rap and dynamic choreography.
