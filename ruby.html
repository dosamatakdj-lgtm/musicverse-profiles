Pre-Debut Story

Ruby began performing in Thailand through dance competitions and school festivals. Her performances attracted attention for their precision and strong stage presence.

After passing a Starship global audition, Ruby became a trainee in Korea. She quickly developed into one of the strongest dancers in the trainee team while also receiving vocal and rap training.
