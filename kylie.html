Pre-Debut Story

Kylie grew up surrounded by music and began taking vocal lessons before becoming a trainee. She auditioned for Starship Entertainment with a vocal performance that impressed the judges with its control and tone.

During training, Kylie quickly became known for her powerful high notes. She frequently ranked near the top of vocal evaluations and was eventually selected as PANDORA's main vocalist.
